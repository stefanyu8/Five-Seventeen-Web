Half a brand costs more than a whole one.

Most owners buy branding in pieces. A logo now, a menu when the printer asks, a set of signs when the landlord approves them, a website when there is time. Every piece is decided by a different person on a different day, and the room ends up saying four things at once.

Five-Seventeen sells one package because that is the only way the pieces agree with each other. You pay once, you decide once, and everything that comes after already knows what it belongs to.
